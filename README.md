## What I'm working on

**[UrgeGuard](https://github.com/GeniusHTH/UrgeGuard)** is my final year project and the thing I care most about right now.

It's a mobile app for people recovering from addiction. The idea is a panic button that doesn't wait for you to be in the right state of mind to press it. It reads signals from a wearable to notice when an urge might be building, walks you through a breathing exercise, and can alert someone you trust.

I went through an addiction phase myself, and I know how hard it is to stop. I built UrgeGuard to help me stay on track, and to make that kind of help easy to reach for anyone else who needs it.

- Built with React Native, Firebase and Android Accessibility Services
- Still a work in progress, and the rough edges are real
